# Methodology
Screening weights: fuel use 17%; operating duration 9%; equipment condition 15%; maintenance gap 14%; stack condition 12%; weather-dispersion constraint 10%; nearby exposure 8%; filtration-control gap 7%; inspection recency 4%; incident pressure 2%; historical signal 2%. Bands: Low 0–24.9, Moderate 25–49.9, High 50–74.9, Critical 75–100.
